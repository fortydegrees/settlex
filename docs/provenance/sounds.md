# Sound sources

The default sound map uses 23 audio files under `public/sounds/`; see
[theme and sound customization](../customization/themes-and-sounds.md) and the
[sound design guide](../agent/skills/settlehex-design/SOUND_DESIGN_GUIDE.md).

The retained synthesis kit has recipes for 11 musical/interface cues:
game-start, your-turn, turn-end, award-road, award-army, discard-required,
resource-blocked, timer-low, timer-critical, game-win and game-lose.
These recipes do not reproduce every encoded MP3 exactly.

Creation sources and terms for the other 12 older effects are not fully recorded.
The kit does not establish provenance for the entire audio library.
[Asset identities](assets.md) and [archived identities](archived-assets.tsv)
record the retained and privately preserved files.
