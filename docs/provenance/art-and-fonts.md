# Artwork and fonts

## Board pieces

David Pratt created the current settlement, city and road artwork in Inkscape.
The 60 SVG files in `public/svgs/pieces/` contain the three piece families and
their colour variants; [the inventory](assets.tsv) identifies them.

## Fonts

The custom display face is built from SVG glyph paths. Its
[source README](../../public/fonts/settlehex-display/README.md) records the
builder and generated concept design, which used Outfit Bold as a visual reference.

The separately bundled Outfit fonts retain their
[SIL Open Font License notice](../../public/fonts/Outfit-OFL.txt).
See [third-party notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for the bundled notices;
this record does not choose a project or custom-font license.
