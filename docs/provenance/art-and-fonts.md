# Artwork authorship and font provenance

## Board pieces

The current settlement, city and road artwork in `public/svgs/pieces/` is
original artwork by **David Pratt**, drawn in **Inkscape**. David confirmed this
on 21 October 2026. The 60 SVG files contain the three piece families and their
colour variants; [the inventory](assets.tsv) identifies their exact files.

Earlier reference studies and discarded experiments are archived privately.
Their notes do not describe the authorship of these finished pieces.

This authorship correction concerns these board pieces. It does not assign a
project or asset licence, or change the separate provenance records for other
artwork, fonts or audio.

## Display font and audio

The shipped custom display face exactly matches the retained v0.4 output, SHA-256 `e5cea15307d2afb7000b14bf31dc35a7fe481088e34cd460e89d4903070dc4df`. Its fontTools builder assembles explicit SVG glyph paths, but the glyph design follows generated concept J using Outfit Bold as its primary visual reference. Manual path construction alone does not establish independent design or redistribution clearance. See the unchanged [font source README](../../public/fonts/settlehex-display/README.md) and [third-party notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). Outfit's separate OFL notice remains intact.

A separate historical match-found calibration plan described synthetic NumPy melodic guides transformed with Stable Audio 3 MLX, followed by audition/mastering. It explicitly did not change production assets; it therefore does not establish the origins or terms of older shipped audio. See [sound provenance](sounds.md).

No overall project license, custom-font license, asset replacement decision or redistribution permission is selected by this record. Preserve attribution while resolving exact source/terms for each family.
